# Missing Features in Mario Game

## Missing Features Compared to Mario Bros 1-1

### 1. Enemy Behaviors
- No implementation of kicked shells interacting with other objects or enemies.

### 2. Level Details
- No underground or bonus areas accessible via pipes.

### 3. Player Mechanics
- No crouching or sliding mechanics.
- No implementation of lives or a life counter.

### 4. Interactions
- Pipes do not allow Mario to enter for transitions to other areas.
- No time limit for completing the level.

### 5. Audio
- Missing background music and sound effects for actions like jumping, stomping, or collecting items.
